h1: Male fertility *tests* and care
intro: Fertility problems can involve either partner, so the Fertility Centre assesses both. Semen analysis is done in the hospital's own laboratory.
sections:
- heading: Why both partners are *tested*
  text: When a couple is trying to conceive, male factors are assessed alongside female factors from the start. This avoids delays and helps the doctor choose the right treatment.
- heading: Semen *analysis*
  text: Semen analysis looks at the number of sperm, how well they move and their shape. The doctor or laboratory will tell you how to prepare for the test and how to give the sample.
- heading: After the *results*
  text: If a result is abnormal, the doctor may repeat the test or suggest further assessment. Depending on the findings, treatment options may include IUI or IVF with ICSI.
- heading: Privacy
  text: Consultations and test results are confidential. You can ask to speak with the doctor privately.
