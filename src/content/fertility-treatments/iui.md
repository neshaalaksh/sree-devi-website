h1: IUI (intrauterine *insemination*)
intro: In IUI, sperm prepared in the laboratory is placed directly in the uterus around the time of ovulation. It is often one of the first assisted treatments a doctor may suggest.
sections:
- heading: How an IUI cycle *works*
  text: '1. **Monitoring ovulation.** Scans track when you are likely to ovulate. Some women are given medicines to help ovulation.

    2. **Preparing the sample.** The semen sample is prepared in the laboratory.

    3. **Insemination.** The prepared sperm is placed in the uterus through a thin tube. This is done at the hospital and usually takes a few minutes.

    4. **Pregnancy test.** A test is done about two weeks later.'
- heading: IUI or IVF?
  text: 'Your doctor will recommend a treatment after tests for both partners. Questions you may want to ask: why IUI is suggested for you, how many cycles to plan for, and when IVF would be considered instead.'
- heading: Your consent and *rights*
  text: Under the Assisted Reproductive Technology (Regulation) Act, 2021, treatment needs your written informed consent. Your doctor will explain the procedure, its risks and what to expect before you decide. The Fertility Centre is registered as an ART clinic (Level II).
- heading: Cost and *estimates*
  text: Costs depend on the treatment plan and the medicines needed, so fees are not listed on this website. Ask for an estimate at your consultation.
